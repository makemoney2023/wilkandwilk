**Status:** Self-authored from the recovered practice copy and the instruction to
use `assets/interiors` as the animation source.

1. **Vibe:** warm, precise, established, playful, quietly premium.
2. **Journey:** enter the practice, feel the care, understand treatment choices,
   meet the doctors, see the office rhythm, request a consultation.
3. **Energy:** calm arrival, building curiosity, lively treatment movement,
   quiet human pause, confident peak, warm resolution.
4. **Feeling:** welcomed, reassured, curious, connected, impressed, ready.
   The remembered moment is the office opening into a layered panorama as a
   single smile line connects every room.
5. **Unique move:** one continuous smile line travels through the practice and
   becomes the consultation underline at the close.
6. **Range:** editorial and playful, with premium restraint.
7. **World:** distinct photographic scenes, not one continuous camera flight.
8. **Assets:** 12 recovered office and people photographs, the Wilk & Wilk SVG
   logo, verified NAP, and the complete legacy copy pack.

## Peak

“It is the orthodontist site where the office opens around you and a smile line
guides you through every room.”

## Signature move

An SVG smile line is drawn by scroll progress across the page. It changes scale
and anchoring in each act, then settles beneath the final consultation CTA.

## Accessibility and performance

- Real headings, paragraphs, links, and reading order remain usable without JS.
- Motion uses transforms and opacity.
- Reduced-motion users receive settled compositions with a native overflow rail.
- Gemini Omni provides silent architectural motion for the waiting-room hero
  (landscape and portrait) and treatment studio. Original photos remain as
  posters and accessibility fallbacks.
- The 600 px legacy photographs are never upscaled as unsoftened full-frame
  detail; layered crops, subtle grain, and controlled image sizing protect them.
- No patient gallery photos are used in the home animation.
