# Types of Braces

## one size does not fit all

At Wilk & Wilk Orthodontics, we recognize that every patient is different and has different needs. As a result, we offer our patients a variety of different types of braces. Some of the types of braces we offer include:

### Master Series® Metal Braces

Metal braces are the most common type and are made of high-grade stainless steel and are small, comfortable, and attractive. We use versatile metal twin braces that provide the three-dimensional control necessary to produce fast, comfortable, and detailed tooth movement. They also provide our patients with the option of choosing coloured ties to individualize their smiles and to have fun during treatment.

### Iconix® AESTHETIC BRACES

Iconix stainless steel champagne coloured brackets truly offer the best of both worlds - the durability of stainless steel and the aesthetics of "white" gold. Similar to the metal braces, these braces provide the three-dimensional control necessary to produce fast, comfortable and detailed tooth movement. They also provide our patients with the option of choosing coloured ties to individualize their smiles and to have fun during treatment.

### Radiance Plus® CLEAR BRACES

Wilk & Wilk uses esthetic clear braces that we feel are the clearest and most esthetic alternatives to metal braces. They seamlessly blend with the natural colour of the teeth and our patients love them! These braces are nearly invisible on the teeth giving our patients confidence in all social settings, at work, and at school. They will not discolour throughout treatment and like the metal twin braces, our patients can also choose coloured ties with these braces to individualize their smiles and to have fun during treatment. This is becoming a big trend in our office!
