# Our Community

## A Commitment to Giving Back

At Wilk and Wilk, we have been blessed with the opportunity to meet and treat so many wonderful people in our local community and are pleased to be able to actively volunteer and to give back to the community in which we live and work. We aim to make a difference by supporting causes and organizations that are important to our patients, our staff, and our communities.

Some of the organizations we proudly support include:

- Cambridge Winterhawks

- Preston Figure Skating Club

- Cambridge Ice Hounds

- Cambridge Jr. Fury Hockey Team

- Cambridge Memorial Hospital Foundation (‘Best Bites’)

- Cambridge Minor Peewee Cubs

- Bridges Shelter Golf Tournament

- The Rotary Club of Cambridge Sunrise

- Cambridge Minor Baseball

- Donna Beattie’s Golf for Charity

- Clemens Mill Public School

- Monsignor Doyle Secondary School

- St. Benedict Catholic Secondary School

- Cambridge Shelter

- Kitchener Soccer Club

- Cambridge Kips Gymnastics Club

- Central Public School

- Southwood Secondary School

- Cambridge Self-Help Food Bank
